# Article 7: answering a worker's request for pay information

Generated by `src/article7.py` from the synthetic payroll, so the figures are the kit's own. A worker asks in writing for their pay level and the average pay levels, by sex, of the workers doing the same work or work of equal value; the employer answers in writing within two months (Directive 2023/970, Art. 7(1) and (4); verification register V06). The category is the one the Article 10 screen uses.

Before sending: check the worker's figures against payroll, have the categories agreed with workers' representatives, and adapt the legal references to the national act. The letter states the right to disclose one's own pay (Art. 7(5)).
