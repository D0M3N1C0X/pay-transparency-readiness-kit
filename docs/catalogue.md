**In the Excel model** (sheet Article 9, column C for the Italian employer; the other employers are the next columns):

```
C9  (a) Gender pay gap, mean hourly pay
     =IFERROR((C34-C33)/C34,"")
C10  (a) Gender pay gap, mean annual pay
     =IFERROR((C36-C35)/C36,"")
```

**The kit's choice.** Reported on hourly and on annual pay, because Article 3(1)(b) defines pay level as both. Pay is base plus every component, annualised for the workers present at the snapshot.

**In a statutory report.** Pay actually paid in the previous calendar year, with leavers and joiners treated as the national transposition says.

**In a statutory report.** National law may define the ranking pay (hourly or annual) and how ties are split.

## (g) Categories of workers, and the Article 10 screen

**Directive.** Article 9(1)(g) for the gap by category; Article 10(1) for the joint pay assessment, owed when a difference of at least 5% in a category is neither justified on objective, gender-neutral criteria nor remedied within six months. How it is computed: [method.md](method.md#the-article-10-screen).

**Result at 30 June 2026.** 12 of 28 categories show a difference of 5% or more, on hourly or annual pay, and need a justification or a remedy:

| Employer | Categories | Flagged | Upper-bound cost to close, a year |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| IT · Milan | 7 | 3 | €512,477 |
| PL · Kraków | 7 | 3 | €674,086 |
| DE · Munich | 7 | 1 | €254,265 |
| ES · Barcelona | 7 | 5 | €472,874 |
| **Group** | **28** | **12** | **€1,913,702** |

**In the Excel model.** Sheet Categories: one row per employer and category, with the gaps, the flag and two columns the client fills in, the justification and whether it was remedied.

**The kit's choice.** Categories come from a gender-neutral job evaluation on the four Article 4(4) criteria, scored per role and banded into points; a flag is raised by the hourly or the annual gap; the cost is a ceiling, the mean hourly gap times the hours of the lower-paid sex.

**In a statutory report.** Categories resting on the employer's own gender-neutral job evaluation, justifications documented on objective criteria before the report date, and any joint pay assessment carried out in cooperation with workers' representatives, as Article 10 requires.
